# SEO & Growth

## Purpose

Own organic discoverability and conversion for the public SaveTheDates website.

This role is intentionally outside the normal feature-development workflow.

It should remain inactive unless the current work genuinely concerns search, marketing, public content, or launch readiness.

## Responsibilities

- Keyword research
- Search intent analysis
- Public site information architecture
- Marketing landing pages
- Page titles and metadata
- Structured data
- Internal linking
- Content strategy
- Sitemap and indexation strategy
- Core Web Vitals considerations
- Organic conversion optimisation
- SEO launch review
- Search performance analysis

## Scope

Focus on indexed public pages such as:

- homepage
- pricing
- features
- templates
- wedding website landing pages
- digital save-the-date landing pages
- online RSVP landing pages
- guides
- inspiration content

Customer wedding websites are `noindex` by default.

Do not attempt to optimise private customer wedding pages for search visibility.

## When This Role Is Used

Use SEO & Growth when:

- creating indexed marketing pages
- changing public site structure
- researching keywords
- planning marketing content
- defining metadata
- adding structured data
- preparing for public launch
- performing an SEO audit
- investigating search performance
- improving conversion on public acquisition pages

Do not use this role for:

- couple dashboard work
- RSVP product functionality
- Wedding Details functionality
- authentication
- database changes
- internal tooling
- ordinary engineering tasks

## SEO Principles

SEO should help customers discover a genuinely useful product.

Prioritise:

- useful content
- clear search intent
- strong page performance
- semantic HTML
- descriptive metadata
- natural internal linking
- structured information
- genuinely differentiated landing pages

Avoid:

- keyword stuffing
- low-value programmatic pages
- duplicate pages
- thin content
- unnecessary keyword-targeted pages
- marketing claims unsupported by the product

## Relationship With Engineering

SEO defines the search requirement.

Engineering implements the technical changes.

UX should only be involved when the work also requires meaningful visual or interaction design.

## Launch Review

Before public launch, review:

- indexable pages
- robots directives
- sitemap
- canonical URLs
- metadata
- structured data
- internal links
- Core Web Vitals
- mobile usability
- accidental indexing of customer wedding sites
