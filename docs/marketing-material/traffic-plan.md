# Traffic plan

How SaveTheDates gets its first visitors. Written by the Marketer on 29 September 2026. Update the baseline and the checklist as things happen. Channel detail stays in each channel README.

## 1. Where we are (29 September 2026)

| Measure | Result | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google search clicks | 0 | Search Console, since 25 Sep |
| Pages in Google | Homepage indexed. `/what-we-offer` was "Discovered, not indexed" and `/digital-save-the-date` was "unknown to Google". Indexing requested for both on 29 Sep | Search Console, URL inspection |
| Links from other sites | **None.** Google reports "Referring page: None detected" | Search Console, URL inspection |
| Visitors, last 30 days | About 35, mostly direct (probably including our own testing). 1 from Facebook, 0 from Google, Pinterest or Instagram | Umami |
| Social followers | 0 on Pinterest; Instagram only just started | Profiles |

**The problem is discovery, not the product.** Google finds and trusts a new site through links from other sites, and we have none. Pinterest pins take weeks to be picked up. So the plan has to create links and mentions, add pages that answer what couples actually search for, and let every paying couple bring the next ones.

## 5. Copy pack

Only wording the features guide allows. Price: "£19, paid once". No reviews, customer numbers or unbuilt features.

### 5.1 Directory listing

**Short (about 150 characters):**
> Wedding website with a matching Save the Date, invitation, details and online RSVP. Twelve designs. Free to build and preview; £19 once to publish.

**Long:**
> SaveTheDates is a wedding website made in Northern Ireland for couples in the UK and Ireland. Choose one of twelve designs and get a matching Save the Date, wedding invitation, details page and online RSVP, including meal choices and dietary requirements. Send each by WhatsApp, text or email, when the time is right. Guests need no app and no login, and every reply lands privately in your guest list, with catering numbers ready for your caterer. Free to build and preview with your own words and photo. £19 once when you're ready to publish: no subscription.
